# Handoff Brief

A Forge app for Jira and Confluence that uses Atlassian's Teamwork Graph to prepare the handoff when someone on a
business team goes on leave, changes role or leaves: what they own, what is at risk, who already works next to
each item, and a handoff page the manager approves item by item.

Status: in construction for Codegeist 2026 (Apps for Business Teams, Teamwork Graph integration). See BRIEF.md.
